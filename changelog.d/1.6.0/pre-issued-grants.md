### Added

- **Pre-issued grants.** `GenesisMeshClient::grants` (`GrantClient`, beta routes) issues, lists, reads and revokes grants (`issue`, `list` with `GrantQuery`, `get`, `revoke`), asks for a holder's time tokens (`request_time_token`), submits a holder's uses (`submit_use`) and reads the revocation feed (`revocation_feed`). The routes answer `404 grants_disabled` unless the NA runs with `EVIDENCE_GRANTS=on`.
- **Acting within a grant offline.** `GrantHolder` holds one grant for the device whose executor key is the grant's bearer. `act` checks each use offline (the capability, the closed envelope with the 1.6.0 number rules, revocation from the feed it holds, the validity, the budget, the uses its time token may still anchor), runs the action (`GrantAuthority`), signs a `GrantExecutionEvidence` chained to the previous use, keeps it in the record outbox and submits it (`GrantActionOutcome::Used`). A request the grant does not cover, a grant not valid yet, or secret material in the parameters is refused before anything runs (`GenesisMeshError::GrantRefused`, `grant_use_secret_material`). `sync` reads the feed, uploads the uses in their chain's order and asks for a new time token; `state` and `restore` keep the holder's chain across restarts.
- **Expiry is not a block.** When a grant has ended, its budget or time token is spent, it is revoked or its bearer key retired, `act` falls back with the request's `GrantFallback` to `governed_action_with_break_glass` naming the capability: the NA's decision when it answers (a DENY does not run the action), break-glass when it does not (`GrantActionOutcome::FellBack`). Without a fallback the request is refused with the reason.
- **Signing the bearer's records.** `ExecutionRecorder::sign_grant_use` (`GrantUseInput`) and `sign_time_token_request` sign a use and a time token request with the bearer key, refusing before signing what the NA would refuse (`grant_use_malformed`, `grant_use_secret_material`, `time_token_request_malformed`); `ExecutionRecorder::public_key` names the key a grant's `bearer_public_key` must be.
- **Grant uses in the record outbox.** `RecordKind::GrantExecution` keeps a use until the NA admits it; `flush_records` submits uses one at a time in their chain's order, after break-glass records and before observations, dead-letters the refusals in `GRANT_USE_PERMANENT_REFUSALS`, and a use behind a dead letter of its chain as `evidence_predecessor_dead_lettered`.
- **Verifying grants.** The `grants` module holds the records' rules: `normalize_parameter`, `envelope_problem`, `in_range`, `scalar_equal`, `normalize_number`, `use_problem` (`UseContext`, `PreviousUse`, `next_previous`), `grant_digest`, `use_digest`, `signed_as_received` and the codes (`USE_REJECTION_CODES`, `ISSUANCE_REFUSALS`). `verify_evidence_events` and `verify_audit_pack` check the entry kinds `grant`, `grant_execution` and `grant_revocation` with the reference's reasons (`grant_missing`, `grant_bearer_mismatch`, `grant_mismatch`, `grant_use_chain_break`, `duplicate_grant_revocation`, then the use rules, the resource position the NA assigned, and in a pack `cited_record_missing`), count them (`grants`, `grant_executions`, `grant_revocations`), accept a judgement `governed_by: grant` and a quarantined grant use, check a grant's policy binding and basis attestation in a pack, and render the pack report's *Grants* and *Grant revocations* sections. `verify_record` verifies a grant, a grant use, a revocation and a time token; `verify::verify_revocation_feed` a feed listing `revoked_grant_ids`; `verify::valid_grant_record` a record's form. The shared conformance suite `grants` runs (85 of its 143 vectors: the 58 issuance vectors are the NA's).
- **`genesis-mesh-verify`.** `record` verifies the kinds `grant`, `grant-execution`, `grant-revocation` and `time-token`; `export` and `pack` check stores and packs holding grants, and the readable output counts grants, uses and revocations.

### Changed

- **The 1.6.0 suites.** The field registry knows the grant records, the entry kinds and the feed's `revoked_grant_ids`; the `audit` suite's record times include a grant (`issued_at`), a time token (`issued_at`) and a grant revocation (`revoked_at`).
- **Governed changes.** `remediation::judgement_state` reads a judgement `governed_by: grant` as matched, as one governed by a prior decision.

### Upgrading

- Update every verifier that reads your exports, packs and revocation feed before the NA turns `EVIDENCE_GRANTS=on`: a 1.5 SDK refuses the new entry kinds and the feed's `revoked_grant_ids`.
- `RecordKind` has a new variant, `GrantExecution`, and `GenesisMeshError` a new one, `GrantRefused`: a `match` over them needs an arm. Record outbox files of a grant use carry `"kind": "grant_execution"`; 1.5 SDKs do not read them. `RECORD_KINDS`, `NA_RECORD_TIME_FIELDS` and `EvidenceVerification` grow with the new kinds.
- Persist `GrantHolder::state` after every use and restore it on start: a holder that forgets its uses signs a second use at a position it already used, which the NA refuses.
